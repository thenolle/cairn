# Cairn

Cairn is a local-first gaming ecosystem. It brings your games from different launchers, emulators and folders into one library, with artwork, collections, save management and optional encrypted sync across your devices.

## Principles

- **Local-first.** Cairn works without an account, without an internet connection and without a subscription. Your machine is the source of truth for your library.
- **Optional cloud.** Sync extends Cairn across devices. It is never required, and private data is encrypted on your device before it leaves it.
- **Honest integrations.** Cairn only offers what a platform or emulator actually supports, and it never bypasses DRM or platform protections.
- **Transparent runtime.** A small background service powers the interface. You can see it, start it, stop it and turn off automatic startup at any time.

## Built with

Bun and TypeScript, using [Gambrel](https://github.com/thenolle/gambrel) for the web interface and native platform APIs everywhere else.
